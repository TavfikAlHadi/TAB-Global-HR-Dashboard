# Interview Request and Consent

## Steps

1. Send the standard interview request template with topic and format.
2. Confirm on-record vs background terms in writing.
3. Record only with verbal consent stated at the start of the recording.
4. Share quotes for factual accuracy check only, never for editorial approval.
5. Store recording and consent email in the interview archive folder.
