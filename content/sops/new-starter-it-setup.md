# New Starter IT Setup

## Steps

1. Receive starter form from HR at least five working days ahead.
2. Create Microsoft 365 account and assign licence.
3. Prepare laptop with standard image and encryption.
4. Enrol device in MDM and enable MFA on first login.
5. Hand over equipment and record asset tag.
