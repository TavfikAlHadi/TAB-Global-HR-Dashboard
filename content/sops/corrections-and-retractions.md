# Corrections and Retractions

## Steps

1. Log the correction request with source, date and the disputed text.
2. Editor verifies against notes and primary sources within 24 hours.
3. Publish a dated correction note at the foot of the article.
4. For retractions, escalate to the Editor-in-Chief and legal counsel.
5. Record the outcome in the corrections register.
