# RadioFinance Episode Production

## Steps

1. Book guest and confirm recording slot two weeks ahead.
2. Send guest briefing pack and technical checklist.
3. Record in studio or remote kit at 48 kHz; keep a backup track.
4. Edit, add intro/outro, and run loudness normalisation to -16 LUFS.
5. Upload to the hosting platform with show notes and transcript.
