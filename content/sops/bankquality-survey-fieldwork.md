# BankQuality Survey Fieldwork

## Steps

1. Confirm sample quotas by market with the survey vendor.
2. Soft-launch to 5% of sample and review completion data.
3. Monitor daily for speeders and straight-liners; remove from sample.
4. Close fieldwork when quotas are met in every market.
5. Deliver cleaned dataset to analysts with codebook.
