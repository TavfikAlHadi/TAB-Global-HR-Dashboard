# Work From Home Arrangement

## Steps

1. Record WFH days in the HR system by Friday for the next week.
2. Stay reachable on Teams during core hours 10:00-16:00.
3. Office managers may set minimum office days per team.
4. Use company equipment and VPN for all work.
