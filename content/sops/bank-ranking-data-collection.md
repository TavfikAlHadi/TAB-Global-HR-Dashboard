# Bank Ranking Data Collection

## Steps

1. Issue the annual data request to participating banks by 1 February.
2. Validate submissions against published annual reports.
3. Flag variances above 5% to the analyst for follow-up.
4. Lock the dataset after QA sign-off; no edits without change log.
5. Archive raw submissions in the research vault.
