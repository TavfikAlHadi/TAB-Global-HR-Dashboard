# Daily News Desk Workflow

## Steps

1. Review overnight wire and regulator releases by 08:30 local time.
2. Assign stories in the editorial tracker with deadline and word count.
3. Writer files draft; sub-editor checks facts, figures and attributions.
4. Editor approves headline and standfirst against the TAB style guide.
5. Publish to CMS, schedule LinkedIn and X posts, log in the daily report.
