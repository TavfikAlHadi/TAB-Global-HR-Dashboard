# Sponsor Deliverables Tracking

## Steps

1. Load every contracted deliverable into the sponsor tracker.
2. Assign an owner and due date to each deliverable.
3. Send sponsor proof of delivery after the event.
4. Close the tracker with sales before invoicing the balance.
