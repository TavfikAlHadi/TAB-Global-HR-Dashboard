# Wealth & Society Feature Commissioning

## Steps

1. Pitch reviewed at the Monday commissioning meeting.
2. Issue a freelance brief with fee, deadline and rights terms.
3. Receive first draft and return one consolidated round of edits.
4. Approve final copy and raise the freelance invoice request.
5. Publish and add the feature to the quarterly content index.
