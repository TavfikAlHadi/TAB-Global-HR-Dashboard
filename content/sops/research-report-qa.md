# Research Report QA

## Steps

1. Analyst completes the self-check list for figures and sources.
2. Second analyst re-computes every chart from the source data.
3. Editor reviews narrative for clarity and executive tone.
4. Head of Research signs off before design handover.
5. Store final PDF and data files with version number.
