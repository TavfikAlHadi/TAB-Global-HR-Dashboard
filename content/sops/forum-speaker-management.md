# Forum Speaker Management

## Steps

1. Send speaker invitation with session brief and logistics.
2. Collect bio, headshot and slides by T-14 days.
3. Hold a 20-minute briefing call one week before the forum.
4. Brief the moderator with running order and speaker notes.
5. Send thank-you and session recording within five working days.
