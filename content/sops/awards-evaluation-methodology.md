# Awards Evaluation Methodology

## Steps

1. Publish evaluation criteria before nominations open.
2. Score submissions independently by two analysts.
3. Resolve gaps above 15 points in a moderation meeting.
4. Present shortlist to the independent panel for final decision.
5. Document rationale for every award.
