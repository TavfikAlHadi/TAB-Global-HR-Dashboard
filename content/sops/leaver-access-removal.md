# Leaver Access Removal

## Steps

1. Disable Microsoft 365 account on the last working day by 18:00.
2. Transfer mailbox and OneDrive to the line manager.
3. Revoke SaaS access listed in the access register.
4. Collect equipment and wipe devices.
5. Confirm completion to HR.
